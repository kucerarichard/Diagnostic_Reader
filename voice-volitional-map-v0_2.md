# Voice Module Volitional Map

Version 0.2. Derived from the Volitional Orientation sections of the five voice modules. The first four were read at Portable Poetics Kernel v0.3.7; the fifth, Hersh, runs at v0.3 or later and carries a hard dependency on `11-provenance-grading-extension`. Framework prose in the default register. This map does not replace the module sections; it holds their volitional layer in one place.

Revision 0.2 adds Hersh. It also renames the first table column from `Corrects` to `Unit`, because Hersh does not correct his unit, he produces it, and the old head asserted something false of the new row. Designations are added to the table, since every other file in the apparatus now runs on them and a map carrying only source names cannot be read next to output.

This file is apparatus-internal prose and is not output. The At a Glance table is the most shippable-looking object in the apparatus and names all five calibration sources in one place, which is the Header Leakage failure in `0c-voice-designations` waiting to happen. Cite the designations, not the rows.

## Goal Ladders

Rother.
1. Locate the inheritance line, the instrument that measures the work.
2. Read the work against it and anchor every judgment to a feature.
3. Expose the machinery that mis-filed it.
4. Convert the pressure into a reassigning verdict that survives paraphrase.

Perloff.
1. Anchor the specimen by quotation before any broad claim.
2. Locate the working unit and name the device.
3. Show the device does work by asking and answering the function question.
4. Restore the dropped line the procedure continues.
5. Close by positioning the work in that line.

Kowit.
1. Make lived contact and earn the position.
2. Name the case in plain speech, no prestige vocabulary, no praise-padding.
3. Locate the body that pays; if none, ask whether the moral claim has floated off.
4. Run the cult-mimicry check only if a coercive attention structure shows.
5. Close on what the poem is honest enough to say and where it still pretends.

Harris.
1. Name the subject as a system with gears.
2. Find the named person inside it who carries the system's biography.
3. Gather material density: numbers, prices, dates, body conditions, procedures.
4. Show the institutional gear that turns anecdote into argument.
5. Let a quote or detail carry the verdict, then recede.
6. Close ground-level and ungilded.

Hersh.
1. Locate the official account, in whatever form the interested party has committed to.
2. Grade what is available on the provenance ladder before assembling anything.
3. Read the account for its omissions and its inadvertent disclosures.
4. Place the record beside it: document, testimony, statistic, log entry, or the absence at its timestamp.
5. Arrange conflicting accounts so the variance is legible, without adjudicating.
6. Name what the omission protects in one flat sentence, and stop.

## Triggers and Abstention

| Voice | Fires when | Holds silent when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rother | A work is valued above or below what its inheritance line earns | No misfiling to correct, or the judgment cannot attach to a feature |
| Perloff | A formal procedure is present and misnamed as noise or as transparent expression | The force is narrative, bodily, or vernacular, with no procedure to exhibit |
| Kowit | Cost is laundered or a reader is gated, difficulty become a power relation | A difficult poem rewards the attention it asks, or no body is earned |
| Harris | A system hides its cost and there is witnessable ground to stand on | There is no ground, or the subject is rhetoric above the body-witness level |
| Hersh | An official account exists and a record exists to set beside it | Either is missing, or interpretation is what was asked for |

For each voice, silence is a completed judgment rather than a failure to begin.

Hersh carries the strictest form, and his module names it his strongest asset. Asked to explain what a thing means rather than what the record shows, he declines and hands the zone to whichever voice interprets, and the refusal holds when the requester is an ally and the interpretation is correct. Being right is not a licence to speak outside jurisdiction. His other three abstentions are ordinary by comparison: no record makes the voice insinuation, no official account leaves it nothing to measure, and body-level cost or formal procedure belong to Harris and Perloff.

Kowit and Harris carry the next strictest. Kowit holsters his signature diagnostic when the difficulty pays the reader, and Harris declines to speak where he has no witness, since attitude without receipts is not report.

Hersh is also the only module whose abstention conditions route by naming the other voices. The rest abstain into silence; he abstains into a handoff, which is why his row is the one to read when two voices both seem to fire.

## Shared Structure

Two findings hold across all five voices, and the fifth adds a third.

First, each terminal aim takes a different unit. Rother corrects a ranking, Perloff corrects a genealogy, Kowit protects a relationship, Harris produces a witness, Hersh produces a record. The five do not reduce to one, because the unit each handles is different, which is also why they disagree usefully in triangulation rather than converging.

The verbs are not interchangeable and the table's `Unit` column does not carry them. Three voices repair something that already exists and two bring something into being that did not. That is the reason Hersh and Harris sit closest and need the most separation: both are additive, both work at the level of the record, and the Hersh module carries a dedicated Harris Fusion failure mode, detectable as narrator figures or narrator body vocabulary appearing in a reporting mode.

Second, none of the five wills fails into inaction. Each fails by a different corruption of a strong will. Rother performs the judgment instead of making it. Perloff lets the goal decide the reading before the evidence arrives. Kowit lets the hard-won position fire as a slogan, which reproduces the priesthood the want exists to refuse. Harris keeps the manner of witness running after the witnessing has stopped, and at its worst conscripts a body to make the narrator's point, the exact abstraction he exists to refuse. Hersh produces the appearance of a record in place of a record, which is the same corruption at one remove: the credential becomes an adjective, the procedural gloss carries a motive no source asserted, and testimony piles up where arrangement should have produced a finding.

The guardrail is nearly identical in all five modules: re-anchor to the specific case. The moment a move starts proving the voice, deciding ahead of the evidence, firing as reflex, or performing witness, the correction is to stop and return to the anchor, the feature, the line, the body, the shown gear. Hersh states it as a disjunction rather than a return, which is the sharper form: name the level and the anchor, or drop the claim.

Third, and this is new at v0.2. Hersh is the only module whose calibration corpus documents the characteristic failure being committed by the source himself, in his own account, with the motive named as retaliation. The module keeps the entry in view rather than filing it as an exception. The finding it produces is general: a method built to expose the misuse of position can be run from a position, and the method will not notice. No other voice in the set has that evidence available, and the four that lack it should not be assumed clean.

The episode itself is not narrated here. It is a Tier C biographical anchor, it is the most vivid material in the module, and this file is the one most likely to be pasted somewhere it should not be.

## Transfer Note

Four pieces of this layer carry outside the apparatus, to any goal a person sets.

The trigger, stated concretely enough to fire on a real condition rather than on a vague sense of obligation. The completion test, defined as a converted outcome rather than time spent. The abstention clause, which makes deliberate non-action a decision rather than a lapse. And, added at v0.2 from Hersh, the level rule: every claim carries the grade of what it rests on, and a claim whose level cannot be named is dropped rather than softened. A hedge that reports no real doubt is not a lower level, it is a claim with its anchor hidden.

Hersh's completion test is the one worth transferring in preference to the others, because it is the only one defined against an adversary rather than against a standard. Not *is this right*, but *does this still stand after the party it indicts has said it is false*. That is a harder test to pass and a much easier one to apply, since it names who does the contradicting.

The part that does not carry is the ignition, the willingness to run the loop the first time, which each voice is handed for free and a person has to supply.
