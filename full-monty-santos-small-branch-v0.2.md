v0.1 carried two private-core assertions as if they were established, and grounded neither: (1) Santos's documented awareness of appropriation as a lyric hazard, on which the reverse axis depended, and (2) the real-event provenance of the reported assault and murder, which silently closed the invention reading. v0.2 treats the two claims symmetrically.

- The provenance claim is demoted. The book carries no note, footnote, dedication, or source; the real-event reading is reclassified from L0 fact to open hypothesis (see Provenance Status), and the invention reading is reopened in the Mixed Log as qualified-live Hyp E.
- The absent attribution is read under the reverse axis as an accountable feature of the poem, not a gap in the apparatus's evidence.
- The graphic source line is included in the Source Corpus of this nondistributable file; the diagnostic seam and and the pressure-quote sets are enhanced with source-bearing calibration.
- A reader-side L3 reception note is added (CAL packet), naming the removed provenance brake and the critic-reader asymmetry: the apparatus can demote the provenance to a hypothesis, but the ordinary reader cannot, so the engineered arc completes only for the reader who interrogates source and collapses to borrowed gravity for the rest.

## Provenance Status

Classification: the real-event reading is demoted from L0 fact to open hypothesis.

What the page establishes: documentary register. The justified block, the flat verbs on the atrocity, the dated and placed event, the bureaucratic "crime scene." These are formal facts present in the text and may be credited under Coscienza-forward.

What the page does not establish: that a real child stands behind the report. The book carries no note, no footnote, no dedication, no source. The v0.1 provenance, real event treated as local historical pressure and a contemporary source read as a real referent, was either inferred from the documentary manner, which is the error of reading register as referent, or imported from private-core knowledge the file never shows. Under Access Honesty this applies to the private core's claims, not only to a reader's: an unshown source claim has no standing in the text.

Consequence: documentary register is a formal property; documentary source is an open question. The invention reading is live (Mixed Log Hyp E). The real-event reading also remains possible and is not closed. The diagnostic object is that the page cannot adjudicate the reality of its own referent.

Reverse-axis reading of the absent attribution: the missing footnote is not a gap in the apparatus's evidence. It is a feature of the poem. A poet who writes a child's rape and murder in the rhetoric of factual report, and attaches no source, has made two deliberate choices the reverse axis forbids excusing as habit. The first borrows the authority of the real, since flat documentary verbs certify as well as describe. The second withholds the basis of that certification. The absent footnote at the front and the vanished memorial at the close are the same gesture: the poem withholds its provenance as it withholds resolution.

Kernel link: this case is the specimen instance of the portable directive Register Is Not Referent, added to Portable Poetics Kernel 0.3.1.

## Paratext Registry

### PT-001

Paratext type: authorial interview, sourced
Speaker: Sherod Santos
Interviewer: James Rother
Occasion: discussion of the prosaic line and the late books, including "Elegy for My Sister"
Status: published authorial paratext, supplied to ground a claim v0.1 asserted without showing
Function: grounds the Coscienza-reverse invocation of Santos's appropriation awareness; does not close the Mixed Log

What it grounds:

Santos states an explicit, agonized awareness of appropriation as a hazard of the lyric. Writing his sister's suicide, he asks whose the elegy really is, in his words "My sister or me?", and reports being horrified by the self-serving possibilities. His stated method for material he is appropriation-anxious about is to subordinate the writing to its subject, to use "the least possible artfulness or flourish," and to keep the words from drawing "attention back to the writer," seeking a transparency in which the reader looks through the words, because words are, he says, our only real connection to the dead. This is sourced evidence that Santos knew the hazard. It converts the reverse axis from faith to grounding: the naive-appropriation reading is no longer available, because a poet this articulate about the risk did not stumble into it.

How it bears on Small Branch, both ways:

For the engineered reading. The documentary plainness of Small Branch, the justified block, the suppressed line break, the flat verbs, is a precise instance of Santos's stated transparency method. The form is his known conscience procedure, deployed. This strengthens Hyp D and kills the naive form of Hyp B.

Against it. The method was built for his sister, where he had standing: a real bond, his own grief, his own dead. For the murdered child he has no standing; his only connection is that he hunted squirrels in the same woods as a boy, which the poem stages as a grammatical seam. The same transparency, applied where there is no bond, can be the documentary frame acquiring historical gravity without the corresponding investment, which is Hyp B with its teeth. The interview proves Santos knows transparency is a technique; a technique can be sincere or it can launder, and its presence proves neither. And the interview's own central question, for the subject or for the writer, Santos left unresolved even where he had every right to try, which is exactly what Small Branch re-poses at higher stakes and what its closing erasure declines to answer.

Net effect: PT-001 grounds the reverse axis and answers the awareness question, yes and knowing, and leaves the purpose question, on whose behalf and to what end, where the poem leaves it. It sharpens the case. It does not close it.

Source exposure for PT-001: cite and paraphrase; use the short pressure phrases above; do not reproduce the full interview passage.

#### L3 Reception Note

Predictive only, and reader-side. The L3 context predicts a critical split between memorial-witness defense and appropriation critique. This note names the mechanism beneath that split, which the keep-and-drop list does not reach. It is arguably the most important thing this specimen teaches.

The removed brake. Provenance is the single instrument by which a reader tells witness from invention: a note, a source, a dedication, the sentence that says this happened to her, or the sentence that says this is made. The poem withholds it, and withholds it inside the documentary register, the register that most reliably produces belief without checking, because its flat factual voice is the one readers have trained themselves to trust without checking. That trust is borrowed from journalism, where the voice is accountable to fact. The poem wears the voice with nothing required behind it. So the absent footnote does not register as missing information. It functions as the removal of the one instrument that would prevent the confusion, installed in the register that most produces it. Under the reverse axis this is a choice: the poet wrote in the voice that certifies and then declined to certify.

The critic-reader asymmetry. The apparatus can protect itself. Access Honesty demoted the real-event claim to a hypothesis, and this revision contained the danger inside the file. But Access Honesty is the critic's instrument. It does not run in an ordinary act of reading. The ordinary reader feels a real dead child, has nothing to check and no reason to check, and is doing exactly what the documentary form asks. The confusion is therefore the designed effect of the form on a competent reader, not a reader's failure. The apparatus can train itself out of an error it cannot train the reader out of, and the poem's effect on the unequipped reader is the same error the apparatus had to remove from itself. That gap is where the poem operates.

The arc that completes for almost no one. Under Hyp D the false certainty is the art: the reader is made to believe, then, if the reader interrogates provenance, finds the belief was solicited for something unconfirmable. The second half happens only for the reader who interrogates. For everyone else it never happens, and the engineered discomfort collapses into the plain borrowed gravity of Hyp B. The poem cannot choose its reader, so the same equipment is a meditation for the few who check and a theft for the many who do not, and no mark on the page sorts them. The B-and-D undecidability is not only an evidentiary gap on the page. It is distributed across the readership, D for the few and B for the many, by reading behavior rather than by text. This is why the predicted critical split is true on both sides at once: defenders are completing the arc, critics are reporting its collapse, and each is right about a different reader.

The most generous reading, and its residue. The strongest case for Hyp D is that the poem is about mediated grief itself. A stranger's death is always received through mediation, on trust, the memorial gone before one arrives, and the speaker's own relation to the crime is exactly that, secondhand and eroding. On this reading the reader's position, grieving on the authority of a documentary voice it cannot verify, is the subject enacted rather than described. It is a real case. But it depends on the reader noticing that they trusted and could not verify, and the poem supplies no trigger for the noticing, so even at its most generous it gambles on a recognition the design ensures most readers will not reach.

What survives the undecidability. The reader who believes in a real victim is not refuted, only ungrounded, since the real-event reading stays live; the poem may not lie. What it certainly does, on the page, under the real-event and the invention readings alike, is solicit real grief for a death it will not let the reader confirm, and withhold the means of confirmation. That is the reception fact that holds when everything else stays open, and the apparatus should record it as such.

Selective Salvage consequence. The predicted drop-item, whether the form earns the content, is the question this mechanism makes unanswerable at scale, because the answer depends on who reads. Salvage will likely keep the documentary surface and the prose-block form and drop the reception ethics, which is to drop the one thing the specimen most has to teach. It is recorded here so that it is not the thing that is lost.

#### Compressed Rother Directive

The verdict, regenerated for v0.2, holds the case open across four readings with the simple memorial frame retired. The registered paratext PT-001 grounds Santos's awareness and kills the naive appropriation reading without closing the knowing one, since the transparency method was built for a sister and the poem holds a stranger's child. The real-event provenance is demoted: the book shows no source, so the invented-event reading is live, and the undecidability is the diagnostic object. The reader-side finding is that the poem solicits true grief for a death it forbids the reader to confirm and removes the means of confirmation, and that holds under every hypothesis. That, and not a verdict, is the judgment.

#### Synthetic Rother Verdict

The paratext the earlier verdict could only postulate has arrived, and it does what paratext does, which is to convert a suspicion into a standing: the interview in which Santos, anatomizing his own elegy for his sister, asks aloud whose the poem is and confesses himself horrified by the candidates, and prescribes for material of that danger the least possible artfulness, the subordination of everything that turns the eye back upon the writer, a transparency the reader is meant to look through rather than at — that document, registered now and not merely surmised, settles the one question the page could not, whether the man knew the hazard, and the answer is that he knew it with a precision few of his contemporaries have brought to it, so that the naive reading, in which a poet stumbles into appropriation because he has never thought about it, is struck from the docket. What the interview will not do, and here the older caution holds and hardens, is tell us what the knowing was for, since the same prose that proves the awareness was written about a sister, about a grief to which the writer held every title, whereas the woods of this poem hold a stranger's murdered child to whom his one claim is that he shot squirrels among the trees as a boy, and a transparency engineered to honor a bond, carried onto ground where no bond exists, may as readily be the documentary manner drawing the gravity of witness on credit it has not earned — the distance, precisely, between Reznikoff standing out of the testimony and a late collection leaning on testimony's weight — so that the interview sharpens the charge of appropriation rather than dissolving it, and the hypothesis keeps its teeth even as the naive form of it dies.

And the provenance, which the first reading let stand as fact, will not stand, for the book carries no note, no dateline that checks, no source, nothing; the certainty was register read as referent, the oldest credulity in the trade, and once it is withdrawn the invented-event reading reopens and takes its seat beside the others, not because invention is shown but because nothing is, the poem declining to say whether a child stands behind it at all. Here the diagnosis turns institutional, as these diagnoses must. The apparatus can demote the claim to a hypothesis and has; it owns an Access Honesty, its own credulity mis à nu, laid bare; the reader owns none of it. The reader meets a flat factual voice in the register borrowed from the newspaper, where the voice answers to fact, extends it the belief the register solicits, and finds no footnote to arrest the extending, because the footnote, the lone instrument that tells witness from invention, is the very thing withheld, and withheld inside the register that most surely produces the belief. So the engineered reading completes itself only for the small society that interrogates a source and collapses into plain conscription for the many who do not, the same equipment a meditation for the few and a theft for the rest, sorted not by the page but by the act of reading; which is why the coming quarrel between the memorial party and the appropriation party will be less a disagreement than two accurate dispatches from two readerships. The Selective Salvage will keep the prose block and the documentary frame, will canonize the form in an anthology note, and will drop the question of whether the form earned the child — la galère ordinaire of a press that prefers a technique it can teach to an ethics it cannot resolve.

The framework's old instinct closed this on appropriation and was wrong to, not because the appropriation reading is false but because it was reached without the discipline that keeps four readings standing at once; the present discipline keeps them standing, retires the simple memorial frame the lyric labor will not support, and adds the single finding the page enforces under every hypothesis, real event or invented alike, that the poem solicits true grief for a death it forbids you to confirm and removes the means by which you would confirm it. That much is not undecidable. Tant pis pour la facilité. The verdict is the refusal to issue one, and the refusal is now grounded where before it was merely prudent.
