Version: 0.2
Kernel: Portable Poetics Kernel v0.3+ (tested against v0.3.6)
Requires: Extended Device Inventory (Sections D and F, Nôm and English-simulation entries)
Module type: function-level trigger-test extension
Status: stress-tested on 29 English specimens across seven poets; all five tests fired positive at least once; four tests recalibrated from calibration evidence

## Module Manifest

Module name: Nôm Function Battery
Module id: device-extension.nom-function-battery.v0.2
Kernel compatibility: Portable Poetics Kernel v0.3
Activation condition: loaded in current chat context
Companion opt-in: English-Nom Hybrid Signals (Extended Device Inventory)
Structural template: Compartmentalization Engine v0.1 (reader-brought lens, named triggers, named non-triggers, period-fit stamp, projection guard)

Function: converts the nine Nôm and English-simulation catalog entries in the Extended Device Inventory from opt-in device names into five function-level trigger tests that run on every specimen by default. The catalog entries name mechanisms; this battery tests for their functions. The [Eng sim] entries already concede the premise: each is a standing admission that the function survives the loss of the script.

Imports:
- Coscienza on both axes, with the Gilson plugin directives when loaded (Critic's Projection Error, Fiber Structure Rule)
- Wyatt Whoso List plugin exports when loaded (steganographic surface, load-bearing foreignness), for the Test 5 boundary
- Period Fit per Checkpoint 2
- Engine vs. Residue
- the Checkpoint Gate (ten steps as of kernel v0.3.6)
- Kernel register and source-exposure rules

Exports:
- five trigger tests with intensity grading
- steganographic architecture discrimination (code-switch / double-surface / declared projection)
- eye-rhyme revaluation note
- Mandonnet streak alarm (systemic) and point-saturation alarm (point-level)
- mechanical margin scan for silent graphemic devices (Test 3)

Overrides: none.

Non-overrides:
- inventory device naming (the battery tests functions; the inventory names parts)
- voice modules inside declared zones
- workshop register lock (test names surface plainly only in Engine Bay and Function Bay; elsewhere the workshop translates them to workshop speech)
- kernel gates, source fidelity, copyright limits

Authority limits:
- The battery reports what fires and what refuses. It does not force a fire to justify its load.
- A battery result is a finding about function, never an attribution of influence. An English poem that fires all five tests owes nothing to Nôm poetics and the report must not imply it does.
- Empty results stay empty. Four silences and one point fire is a complete, successful report.

Module gate:

> What does this battery add that the inventory opt-in does not carry?

Answer:

> The opt-in names devices when a reader invokes them. The battery tests for their functions on every specimen without invocation, with non-trigger conditions that let it stay honestly quiet. The inventory is a catalog; this is an instrument.

## Access Honesty Header

The apparatus does not read Nôm or Vietnamese at evidence grade. Calibration on Hồ Xuân Hương runs through scholarship about her practice, not through the apparatus reading her script. Quotation-before-claim cannot be performed on the Nôm originals, and any Perloff-zone treatment of Hương material abstains accordingly. Every test below operates at function level, which is exactly the level the inventory's [Eng sim] entries certify as script-independent. This header travels with the module: a battery report that omits it when Hương calibration is invoked is out of contract.

## Period Fit Stamp

All five tests are reader-brought lenses calibrated on the practice of Hồ Xuân Hương (c. 1772-1822) as mediated by scholarship, and assembled in 2026. Applied to any specimen, they are retrospective instruments and must be declared as apparatus, not as the poet's frame. Checkpoint 2 applies without exception.

## Grading Scale

Every test reports one of four grades:

- **systemic fire**: the function organizes the poem or saturates its texture
- **point fire**: the function operates at one or few sites, load-bearing at that site
- **faint**: trace present, not load-bearing; logged, reported only in a full battery report
- **non-trigger**: absent, or present only as the confound the test excludes

The point/systemic distinction is calibrated: Beckett's *Enueg I* runs Test 1 systemically; McGrath's *My Sadness* runs it at one point. Same function, different economies. A binary test would have filed them together and been wrong about both.

Point-saturation caveat, added in v0.2. A test can fail by firing at *point* grade on nearly every specimen, not only by firing *systemic* on three in a row. A per-specimen point rate near half across a varied corpus means the trigger is drawn too wide and its fire has lost discriminating value, even though no systemic streak occurred. This is a distinct pathology from the Mandonnet streak and has its own alarm (see Guards). Test 1 exhibited it across three consecutive corpora before v0.2 tightened its trigger.

## The Five Tests

### Test 1. Semantic Dissonance

Source devices: [Nôm] semantic-phonetic dissonance; [Eng sim] parenthetical ghost.
Function: a single lexical unit hosts two or more simultaneous readings that actively resist or ironize each other, and the conflict is load-bearing.

Fires when: the competing senses do not merely coexist but pull against each other, so that holding both at once produces irony, strain, or contradiction the poem uses. The test of a fire, tightened in v0.2: can the two senses be stated as a conflict ("X, but also not-X")? If the senses instead enrich one another without tension, the test does not fire. Clean calibration fires: *flagrant* pinned to the rafflesia (blazing and shameful and, via the *fragrant* ghost, perfumed, on a stench-flower) in *Enueg I*; *lack of attachment* (Buddhist equanimity against literal dispossession by the admired thief) in *My Sadness*; *debauchee of dew* (dissipation against purity) and *awful cutlery* (terror against mundane tableware) in Dickinson; *shrink-resistant heart* (a laundry term seized for emotional recoil) and *patriotic act* (loyalty nailed to betrayal) in the Stand-Up corpus; *electroplated bracken* (industrial process on wild growth) in Muldoon; the stability/fragility split on one color-word in draft calibration.

Does not fire on: ornamental puns; **polysemy in harmony**, where two senses stack, fuse, or enrich without tension (v0.2 flags this as the dominant false positive: pre-tightening the test point-fired on harmonious richness such as *molten blue*, *porcelain* life, and fused senses like *endangered* or *line grows thick*); double meanings available only under reader pressure the page does not apply.

v0.2 recalibration note. This is the perceptual-to-semantic cousin of Register Is Not Referent: ordinary poetic richness (many live senses) was being misread as the specific device (senses in conflict). Across the One Hundred Moderns, Dickinson, and Muldoon corpora the pre-v0.2 trigger ran a point rate near one-per-poem, which tripped the point-saturation alarm. The tightened trigger restricts fires to senses that actively resist each other.

### Test 2. Code-Friction

Source devices: [Nôm] scriptal ghosting; [Eng sim] etymological stratification.
Function: the poem stages a slide between prestige code and vernacular code, and the slide carries class, cultural, or psychic rupture as content.

English ground: the language is lexically digraphic, a Latinate official layer over a Germanic floor, so the Hán-to-Nôm slide has a native analogue. The test extends to staged collisions of any prestige code (foreign tags, learned allusion, liturgical or legal register) with demotic speech, and to code-switched geography, where place names embed a second cultural layer in the map itself.

Fires when: the friction is content, and the two codes stay in live tension rather than resolving into one. Calibration: *Enueg I*, where Latin stage-exit, Provençal genre title, and Homeric grief-drugs priced as pints collide with ganzies and down-and-outs, and the one unarmored vernacular zone (the child at the gate) is the poem's moral center; Muldoon's *Capercaillies* (v0.2 gold standard), where a Norman knight and heraldic blazon crash against Harrods gum-boots and hotel-room push-ups across the whole poem, both registers live at once.

Ruptured vs reconciled friction, resolved in v0.2. Two economies of code-collision must be distinguished. **Ruptured friction** keeps the codes in unresolved tension and fires this test. **Reconciled friction** stages a collision that the poem then harmonizes into a single devotional or unified meaning; it is a real device but a different one, and it fires at *point*, not systemic, with the reconciliation noted. Calibration: Edward Taylor's *Huswifery* sets domestic spinning-craft against sacred vocabulary (distaff, spool, and fulling-mills meeting the Holy Word) but resolves the collision into a single act of devotion, so it fires reconciled-point, not systemic.

Mock-heroic boundary, sharpened in v0.2. Mock-heroic elevation that mocks a small event through single-register irony (Hopkinson's *Battle of the Kegs*) routes to the parody family in Section D and does *not* fire this test. The discriminator: mock-heroic runs one register ironically over its subject; friction-as-content runs *both* registers as simultaneously live. Hopkinson is non-trigger; Muldoon, who keeps both the heroic and the domestic fully operative, is systemic.

Does not fire on: register variety as seasoning; mock-heroic single-register irony (route to Section D parody); accidental register noise in a draft, which is a workshop diction finding, not a device.

### Test 3. Ink-Channel Linkage

Renamed in v0.2 from "Ink-Rhyme." Calibration showed the object is broader than rhyme: any word-binding that lives in the graphemic channel independent of sound. "Ink-rhyme" survives as one member (eye rhyme); the test's true center is the acrostic, the purest case.

Source devices: [Nôm] visual radical coupling / graphemic rhyme; [Eng sim] typographic constancy / anchor character.
Function: linkage between lexical units runs through the visual character-stream independent of sound. Members: word-fusion and space-deletion; acrostic, telestich, and mesostich; anagrammatic threading; sub-lexical letter-extraction and re-siting; eye rhyme (rhyme in ink only).

The channel is the whole test, clarified in v0.2. The binding must live in ink and *not* in sound. This produces two symmetric non-triggers that together define the test:

1. **Not visual manner.** Lowercase styling, punctuation-scatter, and idiosyncratic lineation are visual *register*, not linkage; they do not bind words in the character-stream. This is the ink-channel form of Register Is Not Referent: visual manner misread as visual device. Calibration: Cummings's *somewhere i have never travelled* is non-trigger despite its lowercase-i and parentheses, because its binding is sound and syntax; the name primes a fire the channel refuses.
2. **Not sound-channel binding.** Rhyme, half-rhyme, slant rhyme, and homophonic pun are sound devices and do not fire this test even when dense or virtuosic. Calibration: Muldoon's *Tea* is non-trigger (pure aural rhyme-machine); Dickinson's slant rhyme never fires it.

Grading, calibrated in v0.2:
- **systemic** when the binding is *purely* graphemic with zero sound trace. Calibration and validation case: Muldoon's *Capercaillies*, whose first-letter acrostic spells a full sentence ("IS THIS A NEW YORKER POEM OR WHAT") that is inaudible on the page and exists only down the left margin. This is the definitional center of the channel and Test 3's first systemic fire.
- **point** when fusion binds in ink but half-lives in sound. Calibration: Cummings's *littleliverpill* and *violetflavoured* in *Poem, or Beauty Hurts Mr. Vinal*, where space-deletion does load-bearing work (enacting ad-copy commodification of language) but reads aloud as the un-fused phrase.
- **faint** when a pun or near-pair co-carries in both the ink and sound channels. Calibration: Cummings's *awandering awondering*; Muldoon's *put the pol in polygamy*, a letter-extraction that is also a spoken pun.

Eye-rhyme revaluation, retained and exported. Through this lens, eye rhyme (love/move/prove) is a positive device, rhyme in the visual channel, rather than the failed sound-rhyme English prosody has apologized for since the Renaissance. A specimen using eye rhyme structurally fires this test.

**Mandatory margin scan, added in v0.2.** The highest-value ink-channel device, the acrostic, is the one the reading eye is structurally guaranteed to miss: it makes no sound and does not disrupt left-to-right reading, so it leaves no salience trace for associative reading to catch. This is the residual blind spot (a real structure that is neither faint-on-the-page nor a genre-expected absence) recurring at the graphemic level. Therefore, when Test 3 is live to any degree on a specimen, run a mechanical scan of the first letters of the lines, and cheaply the last letters, before grading. This is a forced check, not a salience-triggered one. The scan is subject to the Weak Signal discipline: a recovered string qualifies only when it survives the pointing (a legible phrase down the true verse lines, not noise from typographic wrap), and a partial hit with the message-words scattered among garbage is pattern-hunger, not a device. The *Capercaillies* acrostic was missed on the first reading pass and surfaced only when a reader pointed at it; the scan exists so the apparatus catches the next one itself.

Note on line integrity for the scan. Run the scan on the poet's verse lines, not on typographic wrap. Source rendering that wraps a long verse line onto a second physical line will inject false initials and destroy a real acrostic; rejoin wrapped lines first. (Rendering, not content: see kernel Rendering Conditions.)

### Test 4. Compression

Source devices: [Nôm] ideographic compression; [Eng sim] vertical compaction / glyph logic.
Function: maximal semantic density packed into a minimal unit, and the unit does structural work.

Fires when: one compact sign (an imported word, a kenning, a forged compound, a portmanteau, a title) carries content the poem then elaborates or depends on. Calibration: *cang* in *Enueg I*, one imported syllable holding the punishment collar that is the poem's whole portrait of consciousness; *Exeo* compressing exit, stage direction, and clinical self-detachment into the opening word; the genre-contract title *Enueg* itself.

Cross-reference: the confining collar as imported emblem links *cang* to the diamond collar of Whoso List (WY-001). Same device family, four centuries apart; the battery logs the family without claiming lineage.

Does not fire on: vivid diction without density (*gunked* is muscle, not compression); specificity mistaken for compression; density as decoration that the poem never spends.

### Test 5. Sustained Double-Surface

Source: Hương's whole-poem practice, the đố tục giảng thanh structure. This test is borrowed from her architecture, not from the script.
Function: the poem maintains two complete readings at once, an innocent surface and a second reading underneath, with total consistency: every image legible in both registers, because one single-register image breaks the structure.

The deniability requirement, added in calibration: doubling alone does not fire this test. The innocent surface must be complete enough to deny the second reading. A poem that declares its wound and then propagates it through every object on the route (calibration: *Enueg I*, whose fled nursing home restages itself in the foaming cloister of the lock) is a declared projection system, structurally doubled and steganographically null. Without this requirement the test misfiles every projection-poem in the language.

Boundary, declared per the Whoso List plugin: code-switch steganography is not this test's ground. In WY-001 the payload is displayed and encrypted by citation, deniability through learnedness, single point of compression. In the Hương architecture nothing is displayed and everything is doubled, deniability through surface wholeness. A specimen concealing by foreign tag, quotation, or learned allusion routes to the plugin's exports, not to this test.

Stakes asymmetry guard: where this test fires, the report must record which economy it found, seal under pressure (deniability with survival value, as in Hương's Confucian order or Wyatt's court) or puzzle for pleasure (the structure borrowed without the danger, as in riddle traditions). Crediting a riddle with a dissident's stakes is projection-onto; denying a dissident's stakes where the page or paratext shows them is projection-against. English native carriers of the structure exist in both economies: the Exeter Book's obscene riddles, blues innuendo under censorship, double-audience lyrics under regimes where speech is evidence.

Fires when: both readings are complete and consistency is total.

Validated in v0.2. Test 5 fired systemic on three Dickinson specimens: *I cannot live with you* (domestic-refusal surface over a complete damnation reading, sustained across every image), *A wife at daybreak* (bridal-morning surface over death-and-divine-union), and *Rearrange a wife's affection* (marriage-language surface over unacknowledged devotion). After 13 prior silences this validates the trigger, not merely the guard. The stakes-asymmetry guard then bit correctly: Dickinson's concealment is psychological and social (a recluse encoding erotic and heretical content in domestic diction), plausibly self-protective, but the page shows no state danger, so the economy was recorded as *seal-under-private-pressure*, not the dissident's seal-under-state-pressure. Getting that distinction right, rather than projecting Hương's stakes onto Amherst, is the guard working as designed. The test also declined to inflate Muldoon's allusive, associative doubling into a steganographic fire (point, not systemic), confirming it discriminates sustained concealment from mere overt or associative doubling.

Does not fire on: partial doubling (three winks and a straight face); declared projection; associative or allusive doubling that is not sustained across every image (calibration: Muldoon); displacement structures, where an announced grief is miniaturized onto safe objects in plain sight (calibration: *My Sadness*, whose elegiac frame runs at t-shirt scale with nothing concealed); code-switch concealment (route to WY-001 ground).

## Guards

**Projection guard (Gilson).** Naming a reader-state is not naming a poem-operation. A test fires on page evidence or it does not fire. Both axes apply: do not credit a specimen with Hương's architecture on atmosphere, and do not deny a fire because the poet could not have known the vocabulary; the period-fit stamp exists so the finding can be made without the attribution.

**Mandonnet streak alarm.** If any single test fires systemically on three consecutive specimens, the battery flags itself for recalibration before the fourth, on the pattern of the schema whose key opens every lock. Non-triggers are findings and must be reported alongside fires; a battery report that lists only fires is out of contract. This alarm fired for real in v0.2 calibration: the Compartmentalization Engine fired systemic on three consecutive Dickinson specimens, and the marginal third fire was demoted from systemic to point under the alarm rather than the lens being trusted at its streak. The alarm working (demote the weakest fire in a streak, do not switch the lens off) is the mechanism behaving as designed.

**Point-saturation alarm.** Added in v0.2. If a single test fires at *point* grade or above on roughly half or more of a varied corpus with no systemic streak, the battery flags that test's trigger as drawn too wide. This catches the pathology the Mandonnet streak alarm cannot: a test that never fires systemic but fires point on nearly everything has lost discriminating value just as surely as a key that opens every lock. Test 1 tripped this alarm across three consecutive corpora and was tightened accordingly.

**Fiber structure rule.** A fire must emerge from the specimen's own thread. If sustaining a fire requires auxiliary hypotheses about what the poem would show if it were doing what the test claims, the fire is dead and the auxiliary count is the evidence.

## Calibration Record

**Hương corpus (calibration source).** Referenced at scholarship level under the Access Honesty Header: the fan, the floating cake, the jackfruit, the paper kite, the poems whose innocent household surfaces hold complete erotic under-readings in full deniability, within a Confucian order that made the deniability survival equipment. No Nôm text read at evidence grade; no translation quoted as source.

**WY-001, Whoso List (boundary case, via plugin).** Code-switch architecture: payload displayed, encrypted by citation. Defines what Test 5 excludes.

**Beckett, *Enueg I* (1935, in copyright, pressure quotes fragmentary).** Systemic fires: Tests 1, 2, 4. Principled refusals: Tests 3, 5. The Test 5 refusal produced the deniability requirement. Also an existence proof of the battery's premise: the specimen is itself built by importing named foreign devices (the Provençal genre contract, the Chinese collar, the Latin exit) into English.

**McGrath, *My Sadness* (2016, in copyright).** Point fire: Test 1. Non-triggers: Tests 2 through 5. Demonstrated the battery's capacity for silence and produced the grading scale.

**Draft calibration (workshop context, unattributed).** Test 1 fire on a split color-word, found by the workshop's Engine Bay before the battery existed. Establishes the battery's workshop use: in draft work the tests run as diagnostic aids under the workshop register lock, and a fire in a draft is a craft finding about the draft's engine, not a verdict.

**Blind stratified corpus, 11 specimens (v0.2).** One Hundred Moderns (Crane, MacLiesh/misattributed, Hopkinson, Taylor, Pound-fragment), Stand-Up Poetry (Trowbridge, Hoagland, Edson, Coleman, Duhamel), and a song-lyric throw-in (Trower). Run blind, no predictions supplied. Results: Test 1 point-saturation surfaced here first (near one point-fire per poem, several being harmony not conflict). Test 4 healthy (systemic on Crane, clean silence across the plain-speech wing). Test 2 held its mock-heroic boundary (Hopkinson routed to parody). Tests 3 and 5 silent but Test 5's refusals discriminated six overt-double architectures from concealment. The song lyric ran all-silent, correct off-domain behavior. Established that Compartmentalization discriminates (no systemic fires) rather than being a runaway lens, contra the earlier Rosselli streak. Also demonstrated the rendering/content split: two specimens had clean rendering but damaged text (corrupt attribution, truncation), drawing no Rendering Note while being flagged as corrupt L0.

**Dickinson, 6 specimens (v0.2, targeted).** Selected to exercise the two then-unfired tests. Test 5 validated (three systemic fires; stakes-guard recorded seal-under-private-pressure). Compartmentalization tripped the Mandonnet streak alarm (three consecutive systemic fires; third demoted to point under the alarm). Test 1 point-saturation reproduced. Test 4 systemic on *The lightning is a yellow fork*. Test 3 still silent, sharpening its status from unexercised to suspect, because even the corpus chosen to break it (Dickinson's dashes and capitalization) failed to fire it: her signature is sound (slant rhyme, hymn meter), not graphemic.

**Cummings and Muldoon, 6 specimens (v0.2, targeted at Test 3).** Cummings three, Wheelwright one, Muldoon two. Test 3 pulled back from the brink and then validated on its center. Cummings's *Poem, or Beauty Hurts Mr. Vinal* gave the first fire (point, on word-fusion) and *somewhere i have never travelled* gave the decisive non-trigger (visual manner refused), proving within-poet discrimination. Muldoon's *Tea* refused a pure sound-rhyme poem and *Capercaillies* gave, on reader prompting, the first *systemic* Test 3 fire, a full-sentence first-letter acrostic inaudible on the page. *Capercaillies* also gave Test 2 its systemic gold standard (Norman-knight-vs-Harrods friction). The acrostic having been missed on the reading pass and surfaced only by a reader is the calibration event that produced the mandatory margin scan.

**Coverage status after v0.2.** All five tests have fired positive at least once. Remaining gaps, held for future calibration: Test 3 lacks a *pure* anagram or acrostic fire discovered by the apparatus's own margin scan rather than by reader prompting (the scan is now mandatory precisely to close this), and lacks a telestich/mesostich fire; Test 1's tightened trigger wants re-running against the corpora that tripped its alarm to confirm the point rate has fallen; Test 2's reconciled-friction grade has one calibration specimen (Taylor) and wants more.

## Battery Report Format

Default behavior: tests run silently on every specimen; fires at point grade or above are reported inside the standard sidecar where relevant; faint results and non-triggers are logged. On request (run the battery, full battery report), all five tests report their grades with page evidence, non-triggers included.

Per-specimen procedure additions (v0.2):
- Rejoin any typographically wrapped verse lines before scanning, so line-initial and line-final scans run on the poet's lines, not the rendering's.
- When Test 3 is live to any degree, run the mandatory first-letter margin scan (and cheaply the last-letter scan) before grading, subject to the Weak Signal survives-the-pointing discipline.
- Report grades against the point-saturation alarm as well as the Mandonnet streak alarm: a corpus-level report names any test whose point rate is running near half.
