Title: The Couriers
Author: Sylvia Plath
Date: 1962
Edition: not supplied by the operator; the poem collected in Ariel, 1965
Provenance: supplied by the operator as a single block of thirteen lines with no stanza breaks; the breaks here are the compiler operator's, restored to six couplets and a closing single line, and are not verified against an edition
Copyright: in copyright
Kind: poem
Unit: line

The word of a snail on the plate of a leaf?
It is not mine. Do not accept it.

Acetic acid in a sealed tin?
Do not accept it. It is not genuine.

A ring of gold with the sun in it?
Lies. Lies and a grief.

Frost on a leaf, the immaculate
Cauldron, talking and crackling

All to itself on the top of each
Of nine black Alps,

A disturbance in mirrors,
The sea shattering its grey one——

Love, love, my season.
