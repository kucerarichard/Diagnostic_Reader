## Source and Provenance

The model comes from tree pathology. Alex Shigo's research for the United States Forest Service, published as *Compartmentalization of Decay in Trees* (the acronym is CODIT), describes how a wounded tree survives by walling decay off rather than healing it. The four-wall scheme and the barrier zone below are Shigo's; the poetic translation is this module's.

CODIT is the source root, not the operating handle. The apparatus names operations by function throughout (Coscienza, Engine vs. Residue, Selective Salvage), and an opaque initialism would be the lone exception, would smuggle the forestry domain into the operating vocabulary, and would be the priesthood gate that the plain-speech directives exist to refuse. The operating name is the Compartmentalization Engine. CODIT survives as the cited source, the way Coscienza keeps Barbi and Gilson, and as the existing device `CODIT sealing behavior` in the Extended Device Inventory.

The decay model rests on one fact worth keeping in view, because it sets the whole frame: a tree does not repair wounded wood. It seals the wound off, abandons the wood inside the seal, and grows on at the margin. The model is triage, not recovery.

## The Model

A wound is an external injury that admits decay. In the tree, agents are fire, weather, insects, animals, and people; decay is the microorganisms that enter through the opening. In a poem, the wound is the loss, death, betrayal, or exposure the poem is built around, and the decay is what enters through it: grief, dissolution, meaninglessness, the rot of the self.

The tree resists in four directions with four walls of rising strength. The strength ordering is itself a reading: it tells you which way a poem defends hardest.

**Wall 1, vertical containment, the weakest.** In the tree, the tops and bottoms of each compartment, formed when the vertical conducting elements plug after wounding. Plug fast and the compartment is short; plug slowly and it runs long. In a poem, Wall 1 governs how long the wound stays open in the poem's own time. Does the injury stay fixed to a moment, or run the length of the text?

> Prompt: How fast does the poem plug the wound, and how far down its length does the decay run?

The complaint loop is a Wall 1 that never plugs. In *Hell*, the damned "live like we do, endlessly complaining"; the want never seals, so the compartment runs without end. Heaven, where "the people are content to let it rain," is Wall 1 plugging fast: the want seals, the compartment is short, the system stays intact.

**Wall 2, inward containment, second weakest.** In the tree, the inner walls, continuous around every ring. In a poem, Wall 2 governs whether the wound reaches the speaker's center or stops short of it.

> Prompt: Does the damage reach the core of the self, or is it held off the center?

**Wall 3, lateral containment, fairly strong.** In the tree, the ray cells, discontinuous, the side walls. In a poem, Wall 3 governs whether the wound spreads sideways to others: the beloved, the reader, the social field.

> Prompt: Does the damage stay with the speaker, or spread to the people around them?

**Wall 4, the barrier zone, the strongest.** This is the load-bearing part of the whole model. In the tree, the cambium forms it after wounding; it separates the wood present at wounding from all wood formed after, and confines the decay to the old wood so the new growth is protected. It is also the plane along which the tree later splits under stress, the defect called ring shake. The wall that protects the future is the future fracture.

In a poem, Wall 4 is the defensive structure the poem builds in response to the wound: a frame, a tone, a closing imperative, a consolation, an ethic. It walls the wound off and lets the poem, the speaker, or the life keep going. And it is the seam the poem cracks along, the place where the consolation meets the void it was built to wall off.

> Prompt: What did the poem build after the wound to keep going, and where is that same structure its fault line?

*Notice* is the type case. The wound is the Levi's that tore "for no reason" and the friend who "halfway home collapsed & died." The barrier zone is the imperative that follows: kiss the earth, be joyful, make much of your time. It walls mortality off and protects the living part. And it is built directly on "you too will one day be gone," so the consolation is the fracture plane: the poem cracks exactly where the comfort meets the death that warrants it, and that seam is the poem's power.

Three further properties carry weight.

Walls are not absolute. The forestry text is explicit: given enough time, even the barrier zone falls. No seal in a poem is permanent, and a reading can name the seal's expiry as part of the structure.

A poem can go hollow and stay alive. When all the inner walls fall and only the barrier zone holds, the tree hollows at the center and grows on at the edge. A poem can carry a void at its core, the dead or the absent, while its surface keeps living. In *The Garden*, the center is the dead beloved, but the surface grows on through her preserved ethic, the garden never weeded because "every creature had as much right to live as we had."

Returning to an old wound accelerates decay. A new wound into an old column rots fast. Canker rot keeps a wound open by killing tissue on a yearly schedule, which the forestry text calls "the same as re-wounding." In a poem, a career, or a field, this is the scab picked on schedule, the injury reopened so the same decay runs again.

## The Engine, at Poem Scale

In Engine Bay terms, the Compartmentalization Engine is the whole-poem drive to seal a wound off to keep the rest alive, rather than to heal or transform it. It is a recognized engine type alongside the Defamiliarization Engine, generated from the draft, never imported.

It qualifies as an engine by the kernel's own test. In *Notice* and *Last Will*, remove the seal-and-abandon move and the poem collapses; what remains is only the open wound. That passes Engine vs. Residue. Where the sealing is incidental and the poem would survive without it, the compartmentalization is residue, and the engine does not fire.

Run it through the standard Engine Bay fields, with the wall map added:

- current engine, the compartmentalization drive in one plain sentence
- the wound, and the decay that enters through it
- the wall map, which walls hold, which fall, and in what direction
- the barrier zone, Wall 4, and its fracture plane
- hollow-but-living, present or absent
- re-wounding, present or absent
- phase-tension mode, after the Mixed Log is tested
- whether the compartmentalization rewards the attention it demands

Test the engine; do not credit it. This follows the Defamiliarization Engine precedent exactly. The operative question is whether the sealing rewards attention or only performs survival. A barrier zone that earns its keep is the engine working. A consolation pasted over a wound it has not touched is the failure. Grant the move neither automatic credit nor automatic suspicion.

Classify the phase-tension mode only after the Mixed Log is tested. Failed System: the compartmentalization is involuntary, accidental, or inherited, a defense the poem cannot help, or a seal that fails and lets the decay spread. Intentional Glitch: the poem engineers the seal beneath a plausible surface, the triage concealed. Transparent Argument: the poem declares the triage as its premise. *Notice* runs in Transparent Argument; it states the seal outright, telling you to be joyful because you will be gone.

## The Same Engine, at Device Scale

The lens that reads a single poem's wall structure is the same animal seen smaller. It is not a separate name. It is the Compartmentalization Engine at device scale, and it lives in the Section H neighborhood beside the Hermetic or Alchemical Chamber Reading, as a constrained opt-in.

Section H already tags devices by containment function: Seals (plausible deniability, credibility management, tonal laundering, `CODIT sealing behavior`) and Valves (the caesura, the dash break, the colon hinge, controlled apertures that let the chamber breathe). The device-scale lens reads which of these devices function as walls in the specimen, and which direction each wall faces.

It adds one distinction Section H does not draw. A seal can be a default wall, structural, present before the wound, or a barrier zone, reactive, formed after the wound to confine it. The existing `CODIT sealing behavior` device is the local instance of a Wall 4. Naming a seal as a barrier zone rather than a default wall tells the reader the poem built that defense in response, which is the difference between architecture the poem started with and architecture the wound forced.

## Calibration Directive

For the kernel's Portable Calibration Directives. Format follows the Defamiliarization Engine entry.

### Compartmentalization Engine

Source: Shigo tree-biology CODIT model; calibration from the Kowit corpus scan.

Directive:

> A poem may be driven by the work of sealing a wound off to keep the rest alive, rather than by healing or transforming it. When the seal-and-abandon is the payload, treat it as an engine, not a device.

Prompt:

> If the barrier zone were removed, the frame or tone or closing move that walls the wound off, would the poem still have something to deliver, or only the open wound?

If only the open wound remains, the sealing is load-bearing and the poem runs on this engine. The engine is produced by devices but is not identical to any one of them: the seals, the valves, the closing imperatives, and the framing moves all serve it. Name the engine at poem scale; name those parts at device scale.

The engine runs in any phase-tension mode. Classify the mode only after the Mixed Log is tested. Test the engine, do not credit it: the question is whether the sealing rewards the attention it demands. A seal that earns its keep is the engine working; a consolation laid over an untouched wound is the failure.

## Trigger and Non-Trigger Conditions

The kernel names Automatic Position as a failure mode: a favorite diagnostic that fires on every specimen. The Compartmentalization Engine carries that risk and must state where it fires and where it does not. The Kowit scan supplies both, including the misfires.

Fires when:

- the poem is built around a wound it walls off rather than resolves
- the poem carries a void at its center while its surface keeps living (hollow-but-living)
- the poem returns compulsively to an old injury (re-wounding)
- the poem turns on a contrast between sealing the wound and refusing to seal it
- a consolation or closing move is built on the void it walls off

Does not fire when:

- the wound is reversed and no decay column forms. In *Joy to the Fishes*, the minnow is saved and tossed back; there is no seal, no abandoned core. The poem closes on contentment, "that minnow is still swimming," which is practical contentment, not damage response. This is the control specimen, the lens's off-state.
- the real engine is coercive attention, not damage response. *I Attend a Poetry Reading* runs on captive attention, owned by the cult-mimicry diagnostic. Forcing the wall model onto it is the Automatic Position failure.
- the poem merely has stanzas or sections. Division alone is not compartmentalization in this sense unless decay is quarantined to a stratum.
- the poem merely closes, or merely concerns loss. The engine is not a name for any closed form or any grief poem.

## Period Fit and Coscienza

The lens is reader-brought and always retrospective. CODIT is 1970s and 1980s forestry science. No poet wrote a barrier zone on purpose, and the model can never be attributed to a poet's consciousness. This is Checkpoint 2, Period Fit. A poem enacts compartmentalization without holding the concept, the way a poem defamiliarizes without holding Shklovsky.

The forward axis follows directly, and the Gilson coscienza plugin sharpens it. The plugin's Critic's Projection Error directive warns against importing the reader's framework as the poet's awareness. The wall structure may be real in the poem; the term is the reader's. Read the seal the poem shows; do not credit the poet with the model that names it.

The reverse axis guards the opposite error. When a poem converts loss into growth, do not launder the wound by celebrating the recovery and dropping the cost. The barrier zone is a fracture plane. In *Cutting Our Losses*, the theft becomes "a sort of aphrodisiac," loss turned to new growth, which enacts the forestry claim that decay is essential to new life. The reverse-axis check asks whether the poem is laundering the theft into romance. The poem survives the check because it marks its own seam: the conversion is "bitter to swallow." A reading that kept the aphrodisiac and dropped the bitterness would be doing the laundering the apparatus is built to catch.

## Relationship to Existing Operations

The engine must earn its naming against the operations it sits near, or it fails Decoration vs. Diagnosis.

Against the Hermetic or Alchemical Chamber Reading. Both belong to the containment family; they answer different questions. The chamber reading asks what transforms inside the sealed vessel; its model is alchemical, and its named failure is the airtight seal that does not cook. The Compartmentalization Engine asks what the poem walled off and abandoned to survive, and what the wall cost; its model is triage. The tell that they are distinct: a seal that holds and abandons its decayed core is a failure in the chamber reading and the system working in this one. The engine requires no transformation at all.

Against the complaint-loop directive. The engine is the mechanism beneath it. The complaint loop is a Wall 1 that never plugs. The directive names the loop tonally and morally; the engine names it architecturally. Build the engine and the complaint loop gains a structural floor.

Against Selective Salvage. They overlap on institutional sealing. In *Fragment of Ancient Skull*, institutional time walls the man's death off so completely that the human particulars drop out, "the fate of his calico, the anguish of his father, the whereabouts of his hat," and only the labeled artifact survives. Selective Salvage names the keep-list and the drop-list. The engine adds the mechanism: institutional time as a barrier zone that confines the human and lets only the fragment through. When both fire, the engine supplies the mechanism, Selective Salvage supplies the audit.

Against the cult-mimicry diagnostic. They are orthogonal and can co-fire. Cult-mimicry reads coercive attention structure; the engine reads damage-response structure. The poems split cleanly: *I Attend a Poetry Reading* and *Lurid Confessions* are cult-mimicry; *Notice*, *Last Will*, and *Cutting Our Losses* are the engine; *Hell* is both, a failed compartmentalization and a moral-attention failure.

Against trauma theory and Coleridgean organic form. Trauma theory rhymes on one point, re-wounding resembles traumatic repetition, but it is psychoanalytic and event-centered, with no compartmentalization-as-survival, no barrier-zone cost, no hollow-but-living. The engine is, against the Coleridgean tradition, an anti-organicist organic model: the tree is many trees, and it survives by abandoning its parts. That gives the engine a polemical position rather than a free-floating metaphor, but the position is the reader's frame, not the poet's, and the period-fit guard holds.

## Calibration Cases

From the Kowit corpus scan. Pressure quotes only, smallest unit.

*Notice*. The type case. Wound: the Levi's torn "for no reason," the friend dead "halfway home." Barrier zone: the imperative to "kiss the earth & be joyful," built on "you too will one day be gone." Wall 4 is the fracture plane; the consolation cracks along the death. Mode: Transparent Argument.

*Last Will*. The inverse. The speaker refuses every wall, demanding the universe go dark because "what possible reason could life in any form care to exist?" once he is gone. Decay with no barrier zone, the self insisting its rot spread without limit. The case that gives Kowit's "silliness of the ego" a mechanism: ego is the refusal to let your own decay be compartmentalized.

*Hell*. The complaint loop as a failed Wall 1, the want that never plugs; heaven as Wall 1 plugging. The poem holds the engine's two states against each other.

*Cutting Our Losses*. Decay essential to new life, the theft turned aphrodisiac, with the reverse-axis seam marked: "bitter to swallow."

*The Garden*. Hollow-but-living. The dead beloved at the center, the surface growing on through her preserved ethic.

*Joy to the Fishes*. Non-fire. The wound reversed, the minnow saved, the close on contentment. The control specimen.

*I Attend a Poetry Reading*. Non-fire. Captive attention, owned by cult-mimicry. Forcing the engine here is the Automatic Position failure.

## Failure Modes

Automatic Position. The engine fires on every poem about loss or every closed form. Correction: run the non-trigger list. Confirm a wound walled off, not merely a closure or a grief.

Decoration over Diagnosis. The wall vocabulary is laid over a poem it does not illuminate. Correction: ask whether the wall map reveals a mechanism a plainer reading would miss. If not, drop the engine and read plainly.

Forestry-metaphor drift. The reading elaborates the tree biology past the point where it tracks the poem. Correction: the model serves the specimen. When the cambium and the ring shake stop naming something on the page, stop naming them.

Laundering through the barrier zone. A reading celebrates a poem's recovery and drops the cost the seal carries. Correction: run the reverse axis. Name the fracture plane.

Crediting the poet with the model. A reading treats the wall structure as the poet's conscious design. Correction: Period Fit and the Critic's Projection Error directive. The structure is real; the term is the reader's.

## File End Test

The module succeeds when a reading can name a poem's wound, its walls, and its barrier zone in plain terms; can tell a sealing that rewards attention from a consolation pasted over an untouched wound; can let the engine stay silent on poems whose wound is reversed or whose drive is coercive attention; and can hold the period-fit line, reading the seal the poem shows without crediting the poet with the model that names it.

It fails when the wall vocabulary decorates a reading rather than revealing a mechanism, when the forestry metaphor runs past the poem, or when the engine becomes the automatic position it was built to guard against.

## Glossary

- **CODIT**: Compartmentalization Of Decay In Trees. Alex Shigo's forestry model, the source root for this module. Not the operating name.
- **Barrier zone**: the strongest wall (Wall 4), formed after wounding, confining decay to the wood present at the time of the wound. The poem's post-wound defensive structure, and its fracture plane.
- **Ring shake**: the defect in which wood splits along the barrier zone under stress. The seam where a poem's consolation cracks along the wound it walls off.
- **Hollow-but-living**: a poem carrying a void at its core while its surface keeps living, the structural analogue of a hollow tree alive at the margin.
- **Re-wounding**: returning to an old injury, which accelerates decay. The scab picked on schedule.
- **Compartment**: in the tree, the least unit of containment. In the poem, a stratum to which decay is confined.
