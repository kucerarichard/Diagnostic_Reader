# Protocol change: Muse reader/evaluator

Effective in this snapshot: Gemini is removed from the active regression workflow.

1. Muse Chat 1 is the reader/test subject.
2. Muse Chat 2 is the evaluator, in a separate fresh chat.
3. ChatGPT is the adjudicator, in a separate chat.

The two Muse stages are role-separated but not model-family independent. Record exact visible model/version and chat conditions. Do not claim cross-model replication from this protocol. Existing result records from a Gemini run, if any, remain historical and must not be relabeled as Muse results.
